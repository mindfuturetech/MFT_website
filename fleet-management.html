Frequently Asked Questions

How does fleet management software work?

Fleet management software uses GPS tracking, telematics, and AI analytics to monitor vehicles, optimize routes, schedule maintenance, and improve operational efficiency.

What are the benefits of fleet management?

Benefits include reduced operating costs, improved efficiency, enhanced safety, better compliance, and data-driven decision making.

Can I track my vehicles in real-time?

Yes, our platform provides real-time GPS tracking with live map dashboards, geofencing, and instant alerts for any unauthorized movements.

How does maintenance scheduling work?

Our system automatically tracks vehicle service history and sends reminders for upcoming maintenance based on mileage, time, or usage patterns.

Is there a mobile app for drivers?

Yes, we offer a mobile app for drivers that provides real-time updates, communication with dispatch, and easy access to routes and assignments.

How can fleet management reduce costs?

By optimizing routes, reducing fuel consumption, preventing breakdowns, and improving overall fleet efficiency, our platform helps reduce operational costs by up to 30%.
