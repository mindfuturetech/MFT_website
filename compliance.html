FAQ

Commonly Asked Questions

How can fleets streamline compliance?

By using an integrated platform that combines HOS, DVIR, and IFTA in one view with automated reporting and real-time alerts.

What is fleet compliance?

Fleet compliance refers to adhering to regulations set by authorities like FMCSA, including hours of service, vehicle inspections, and fuel tax reporting.

Why is compliance important?

Compliance ensures driver safety, avoids costly fines, protects your CSA score, and maintains your company's reputation and operational efficiency.

What is HOS compliance?

Hours of Service compliance ensures drivers follow regulations on driving hours, rest breaks, and work limits to prevent fatigue-related accidents.

What is DVIR?

Driver Vehicle Inspection Reports are required pre-trip and post-trip inspections that document vehicle condition and identify any safety issues.

What is IFTA?

The International Fuel Tax Agreement simplifies fuel tax reporting for interstate carriers by consolidating reporting across multiple jurisdictions.
