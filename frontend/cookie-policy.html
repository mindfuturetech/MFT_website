Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

In short: We use cookies to personalize content and improve performance. You can accept, reject, or customize which cookie categories we use. Change your choice anytime via the banner below or in your browser settings.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your actions and preferences over time, so you don't have to re-enter them each time you return.

We also use similar technologies such as local storage, pixels, and web beacons. For simplicity, all of these are referred to as "cookies" in this policy.

2. How We Use Cookies

  • Remember your cookie consent choice
  • Personalize content and remember your preferences
  • Understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it
  • Measure the performance of our pages and features
  • Keep our website secure and prevent fraud

3. Types of Cookies We Use

a. Strictly Necessary Cookies

Essential for the website to function and cannot be switched off. They are usually set in response to actions you take, such as setting your cookie preferences.

b. Performance & Analytics Cookies

These help us understand how visitors interact with our site by collecting anonymous information. They are only set if you click Allow all or enable them in Customize.

c. Functionality Cookies

These allow the site to remember choices you make (like your language or region) to provide a more personalized experience.

d. Marketing Cookies

These may be set by our advertising partners to build a profile of your interests and show relevant ads on other sites. They are only set with your consent.

5. Third-Party Cookies

6. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

a. Our cookie banner

When you first visit, a banner appears with three options: Reject all, Accept all, and Customize. Your choice is saved and remembered on future visits. You can also re-open the banner by visiting this page.

b. Browser settings

You can block or delete cookies through your browser:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

c. Opt out of analytics

You can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on to prevent your data from being used by Google Analytics.

Note: Disabling certain cookies may affect how the website functions. Strictly necessary cookies cannot be disabled through our banner.
