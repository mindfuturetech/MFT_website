What Is Smart Dairy?

Smart Dairy combines IoT wearables, AI analytics, automated milking, and data management to help dairy farms make data-driven decisions — improving yield, reducing waste, and ensuring sustainable dairy practices.

Herd Monitoring

Track animal health, activity levels, and rumination with real-time smart collars and sensors.

Reduce feed waste, water usage, and energy costs with AI-driven operational recommendations.

Yield Forecasting

Predict milk production outcomes with machine learning models for better supply chain planning.
