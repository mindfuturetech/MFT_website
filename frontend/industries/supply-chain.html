FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers on transportation management, logistics optimization and supply chain visibility.

What is a Transportation Management System (TMS)?

A TMS plans, executes and tracks the movement of goods. It covers route planning, carrier management and rating, real-time shipment tracking, load and capacity management and freight audit and payment.

How does AI optimize routes and loads?

AI route optimization, dynamic load planning and dynamic rerouting find efficient plans and adjust them as conditions change, helping reduce cost and deliver faster.

What does end-to-end supply chain visibility mean?

It means you can follow goods from supplier to customer, with real-time shipment tracking, event monitoring, predictive ETA calculations, exception alerts and customer delivery notifications.

Can it manage multiple carriers and transport modes?

Yes. Multi-modal transportation and carrier management let you plan across modes and compare carriers in one system, with supplier performance tracking.

Can it help us reduce fuel use and track our carbon footprint?

Yes. Fuel optimization helps lower consumption, and carbon footprint tracking gives you the data to measure and report your environmental impact.
