FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about core banking, risk management and digital payments.

What is a cloud-native core banking platform?

The cloud-native modern banking core built on microservices and cloud infrastructure instead of a legacy monolith. It supports open banking APIs, real-time transaction processing and multi-currency operations, and scales as your institution grows.

How does AI help with fraud detection and risk management?

AI models monitor transactions in real time, using behavioral analytics and anomaly detection to flag suspicious activity, and support credit risk assessment so decisions are faster and better informed.

Do you support AML, KYC and regulatory reporting?

Yes. Our risk and compliance solutions automate AML and KYC checks and regulatory reporting, and regulatory compliance is built into the core banking platform.

Which payment methods can be supported?

We support card and wallet processing, real-time payments, and UPI and ACH integration across multiple channels. Our payment solutions are built to be PCI DSS compliant.

Can we move off a legacy system without disrupting operations?

Our microservices-based design lets you modernize in stages rather than all at once. The migration approach depends on your systems and risk appetite, so we plan it with you during discovery.
