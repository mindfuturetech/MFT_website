FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about running a smarter resort with modern hospitality technology.

What is resort management software?

It is a connected set of tools that run guest-facing and back-office operations: reservations, check-in and check-out, dining and activity bookings, housekeeping and guest feedback.

What does a Property Management System (PMS) do for a resort?

A PMS is the central hub for your property. It manages reservations, room status and guest records, so every department works from the same up-to-date information.

How does smart check-in and check-out improve the guest experience?

Guests spend less time at the front desk and more time enjoying the resort, while your staff handle fewer repetitive tasks.

Can guests book dining and activities digitally?

Yes. Dining and activity bookings can be made digitally, making it easy for guests to plan their stay and helping you manage capacity and demand.

How do housekeeping automation and guest feedback analytics help?

Housekeeping automation keeps rooms ready faster, and guest feedback analytics show what guests love and where to improve, so you can raise satisfaction and make better decisions.
