Overview

What Is IAM?

Identity and Access Management (IAM) is the framework of policies, processes, and technologies that ensure the right people have the right access to the right resources — and everyone else is denied.

Who Are You?

Authenticate users with multi-factor methods — passwords, biometrics, tokens.

What Can You Access?

Authorize users with role-based and attribute-based access controls.

What Did You Do?

Audit every access with logs, reports, and compliance-ready trails.
