FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently asked questions about EHR, telemedicine and AI in clinical care.

What is an Electronic Health Records (EHR) system?

An EHR digitizes patient records and clinical documentation in one secure place. It also covers e-prescribing and medication management, lab and imaging integration, and billing and insurance claims.

What can a telemedicine platform do?

It supports HD video consultations, virtual waiting rooms, secure messaging, e-prescriptions, appointment scheduling and remote patient monitoring, so patients can get care without traveling.

How is patient data kept secure and compliant?

Our health care solutions are designed to be HIPAA compliant and secure. The exact compliance requirements for your region and organization are reviewed with you during planning.

How does AI support clinical decisions?

AI-powered tools offer diagnostic assistance, predictive analytics, risk stratification and drug interaction alerts. They support clinicians with insights and do not replace their judgment.

Can it connect with our labs, imaging and billing?

Yes. The EHR includes lab and imaging integration and billing and insurance claims, so information flows between departments instead of being re-entered.
