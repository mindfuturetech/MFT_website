FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what restaurant owners ask about POS, kitchen systems and online ordering.

What is restaurant management software?

It is an integrated system that connects your POS, kitchen display, online orders, table reservations, inventory and loyalty programs, so front of house and kitchen stay in sync.

What is a Kitchen Display System (KDS) and how does it speed up service?

A KDS shows orders to kitchen staff on a screen as soon as they are placed, replacing paper tickets. Orders are clearer and easier to prioritize, which helps you serve more customers.

Can we take online orders and table reservations?

Yes. Online ordering and table and reservation management let guests order and book from their phones, and everything flows into the same system your staff already use.

How do inventory tracking and recipe costing reduce costs?

Inventory and recipe costing show what each dish costs to make and how much stock you use, so you can control waste, price your menu with confidence and reduce costs.

Does it support customer loyalty programs?

Yes. Customer loyalty programs reward repeat diners and encourage them to come back, helping you grow revenue from the guests you already have.
