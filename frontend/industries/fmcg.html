FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything FMCG brands and manufacturers ask about supply chain, retail execution and consumer insights.

What does technology for FMCG companies include?

It covers three areas: supply chain optimization, retail execution in stores and field, and consumer intelligence. Together they help you get the right product to the right shelf and understand who buys it.

How can technology help us reduce stockouts?

AI-powered demand forecasting, real-time inventory visibility and warehouse automation help you anticipate demand and keep stock where it is needed, so shelves stay full.

What is perfect store execution and how do field apps support it?

It means every store follows your planogram and promotions. Mobile-first field apps support image-recognition shelf audits, planogram compliance tracking and order capture, with real-time reporting for managers.

Can we monitor the cold chain and optimize delivery routes?

Yes. Cold chain monitoring tracks temperature-sensitive goods, while route and logistics optimization reduces transit time and cost.

How do loyalty programs and consumer analytics work?

Consumer behavior analytics and customer segmentation reveal what buyers want. You can then run loyalty and rewards programs and personalized, omnichannel marketing to build repeat purchases.
