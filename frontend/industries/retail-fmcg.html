FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What retailers and FMCG sellers ask most about POS, inventory and omnichannel selling.

What is retail and FMCG technology?

It is the set of tools that run a modern store and its supply chain: smart checkout, real-time inventory, loyalty programs, demand forecasting and analytics dashboards, across physical and online channels.

How does real-time inventory reduce stockouts and shrinkage?

Real-time inventory shows exactly what is on hand in every location, so you can restock before items run out and spot discrepancies that point to shrinkage.

What does a smart POS do beyond billing?

Smart POS handles fast checkout and also connects to inventory, customer loyalty programs and analytics, so each sale updates stock and builds your customer insights.

How does demand forecasting help my store?

Demand forecasting uses sales data to predict what customers will buy, so you can order the right quantities and avoid both overstocking and missed sales.

Can we sell across stores and online with one system?

Yes. The omnichannel experience and supply chain integration keep inventory, customers and orders connected across your stores and online channels.
