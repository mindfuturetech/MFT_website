FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About AI Smart Cameras

Quick answers to the most common questions about AI video analytics, edge processing, and smart surveillance.

How accurate are AI smart cameras?

Our AI smart cameras achieve up to 99.9% detection accuracy for people, vehicles, and objects under standard conditions. Accuracy is maintained by running deep-learning models directly on the camera (edge AI), which reduces latency and avoids the bandwidth and compression issues that affect cloud-only video analytics.

Do smart cameras work without internet?

Yes. Because AI processing happens on the camera itself, core detection, alerting, and local recording continue to work even if the internet connection drops. Cloud dashboards, remote viewing, and cross-site analytics resume automatically when connectivity returns.

What is edge processing in smart cameras?

Edge processing means the AI model runs directly on the camera hardware instead of sending every frame to a cloud server. This lowers latency (alerts in under a second), improves privacy (video doesn't leave the device unless you choose), and significantly reduces bandwidth and storage costs.

Can smart cameras recognize faces and license plates?

Yes. Our AI smart cameras support facial recognition (for employees, VIPs, or flagged individuals) and license plate recognition (for access control and vehicle tracking). Both features run on-device and can trigger real-time alerts or open gates automatically.

How many traditional cameras can one AI smart camera replace?

In typical deployments, one AI smart camera provides roughly 10x the coverage of a traditional fixed camera, because it can pan, detect multiple object types simultaneously, and analyze behavior — reducing the total number of cameras, cabling, and monitoring staff required.

Is AI video surveillance privacy-compliant?

Edge AI improves privacy compliance because video is processed locally and only metadata or alerts are transmitted by default. Facial recognition and license plate data can be configured to comply with GDPR, DPDP, and other regional regulations, including retention limits and on-premise storage options.
