Overview

What Is RFID?

RFID (Radio Frequency Identification) uses radio waves to automatically identify and track tags attached to objects. Unlike barcodes, RFID doesn't require line-of-sight and can read hundreds of tags simultaneously.

Contactless Reading

Read tags without line-of-sight, from a distance, even through materials.

Bulk Scanning

Scan hundreds of items simultaneously — no more one-by-one barcode scans.

Real-Time Tracking

Know the exact location and status of every tagged asset at all times.
