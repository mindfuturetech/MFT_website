FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About RFID

Quick answers about RFID technology, inventory tracking, accuracy, and integrations.

What is RFID and how does it work?

RFID (Radio Frequency Identification) uses radio waves to automatically identify and track tags attached to objects. A reader sends a radio signal to a tag, and the tag responds with its unique ID. Unlike barcodes, RFID doesn't require line-of-sight and can read hundreds of tags simultaneously.

How does RFID compare to barcode scanning?

RFID reads tags without line-of-sight, from greater distances, and can scan hundreds of items simultaneously — while barcodes must be scanned one at a time with a direct line of sight. This makes RFID up to 10x faster for inventory counts and far more accurate for bulk operations.

What industries use RFID tracking?

RFID is used across retail, warehousing, manufacturing, logistics, healthcare, agriculture, and asset-intensive industries. Common applications include inventory management, asset tracking, supply chain visibility, access control, anti-theft protection, and equipment maintenance tracking.

Can RFID work through walls, boxes, or containers?

Yes. RFID signals can pass through many materials including cardboard, plastic, and fabric — allowing tags to be read inside boxes, pallets, and containers without opening them. Metal and liquids can reduce range, so we recommend specialized tags for those environments.

How accurate is RFID inventory tracking?

RFID inventory tracking typically achieves 99%+ accuracy — significantly higher than manual or barcode-based counting. Accuracy depends on tag placement, reader positioning, and the operating environment, which we assess during deployment to maximize read rates.

Does RFID integrate with existing ERP and WMS systems?

Yes. RFID readers and middleware integrate with ERP, WMS, and inventory management systems through standard APIs. Tag events flow into your existing systems in real time, so you don't need to replace your infrastructure — you add real-time visibility on top of it.
