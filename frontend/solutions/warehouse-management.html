FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Warehouse Management

Quick answers about WMS, inventory accuracy, multi-warehouse operations, integrations, and automation.

What is a Warehouse Management System (WMS)?

A Warehouse Management System (WMS) is software that controls and optimizes every operation in a warehouse — from receiving and put-away to picking, packing, and shipping. It provides real-time inventory visibility, directs worker tasks, integrates with automation, and connects to ERP and TMS systems.

How does WMS improve inventory accuracy?

WMS improves accuracy through real-time tracking of every movement, barcode and RFID scanning at each step, cycle counting, and automated reconciliation. This typically achieves 99.9% inventory accuracy and eliminates the manual errors common in spreadsheet-based tracking.

Can WMS handle multiple warehouses?

Yes. Our WMS supports multi-warehouse operations from a single dashboard — with per-location inventory, cross-warehouse transfers, location-level reporting, and centralized visibility. This lets you manage a distributed network as easily as a single facility.

Does WMS integrate with ERP and transportation systems?

Yes. WMS integrates with ERP systems for order and inventory sync, and with TMS (transportation management systems) for shipping and carrier coordination. Standard APIs and pre-built connectors support SAP, Oracle, Dynamics, and most major ERPs.

How does WMS reduce labor costs?

WMS reduces labor costs by optimizing pick paths, batch and wave picking, automated task assignment, and real-time productivity tracking. Combined, these typically cut labor costs by 20–30% while improving throughput by up to 50%.

Can WMS integrate with warehouse automation?

Yes. WMS integrates with conveyors, AS/RS (automated storage and retrieval), AMRs (autonomous mobile robots), pick-to-light systems, and voice picking. Automation directives are managed centrally by WMS to coordinate both human workers and robotic systems.
