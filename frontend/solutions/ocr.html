FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About OCR

Quick answers about AI document automation, accuracy, languages, integrations, and security.

What is OCR and how does it work?

OCR (Optical Character Recognition) converts images of text — scanned documents, photos, PDFs — into machine-readable text. AI-powered OCR goes further: it understands document context, extracts structured fields (invoice number, amount, tax, ID details), and pushes the data into your systems automatically.

How accurate is AI-powered OCR?

Our AI-powered OCR achieves 99%+ accuracy on printed documents and high accuracy on handwritten and low-quality scans. Accuracy improves further through field-level validation rules that catch and correct common extraction errors.

Which document types can the OCR process?

The OCR processes invoices, receipts, purchase orders, bank statements, forms, passports, driving licenses, ID cards, contracts, and scanned PDFs. It handles both printed and handwritten text, and supports batch processing for high-volume workflows.

Does the OCR support multiple languages?

Yes. The OCR supports 50+ languages, including English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Arabic, Chinese, and major European languages. Language detection is automatic, so mixed-language documents are processed correctly.

Can OCR integrate with our ERP or CRM?

Yes. Extracted data can be pushed directly into your ERP, CRM, accounting software, or database through standard APIs. This lets invoice data flow into accounting, ID data into KYC systems, and form data into any downstream workflow — without manual entry.

Is OCR secure for sensitive documents?

Yes. Documents are processed over encrypted connections, and data is encrypted at rest. We support on-premise and private-cloud deployment for industries that require data residency, and all access is logged with full audit trails.
