FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About FinConnect

Quick answers about unified financial operations, reconciliation, integrations, and compliance.

What is FinConnect?

FinConnect is an integrated financial technology platform that connects CA, CS, and clients in one system. It unifies payments, automated reconciliation, real-time cash flow, accounting integrations, smart invoicing, and GST/TDS compliance reporting — replacing scattered tools and spreadsheets.

How does automated reconciliation work?

FinConnect automatically matches transactions across your bank accounts, payment gateways, and accounting ledgers. It flags mismatches for review and reconciles the rest in seconds — typically making reconciliation 5x faster and reducing manual errors to near zero.

Which accounting software does FinConnect integrate with?

FinConnect integrates with Tally, QuickBooks, Zoho Books, and major ERPs through standard APIs. Financial data flows both ways, so your books stay in sync without manual exports or double entry.

Can FinConnect handle GST and TDS compliance?

Yes. FinConnect generates GST, TDS, and other regulatory reports with one click, based on live transaction data. It keeps filings accurate and audit-ready, and reduces the manual effort typically spent on monthly and quarterly compliance.

Is FinConnect suitable for CA and CS firms managing multiple clients?

Yes. FinConnect is built for CA and CS firms managing multiple client accounts. Each client gets an isolated workspace, and the firm gets a consolidated dashboard for reconciliation status, compliance deadlines, and cash flow across all clients.

What payment methods does the FinConnect gateway support?

The payment gateway supports cards, UPI, mobile wallets, net banking, and other digital methods. All payments flow into a single ledger for reconciliation, so you don't need to reconcile each payment method separately.
