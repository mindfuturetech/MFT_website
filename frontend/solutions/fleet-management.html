FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Fleet Management

Quick answers about fleet tracking, dispatch, fuel savings, driver safety, and compliance.

What is fleet management software?

Fleet management software is a platform that oversees every aspect of your vehicles — dispatch, routing, maintenance, fuel, driver performance, compliance, and analytics. It gives you one dashboard to track vehicles in real time, optimize operations, and reduce costs across your entire fleet.

How does GPS fleet tracking work?

GPS fleet tracking uses telematics devices installed in each vehicle to transmit live location, speed, fuel level, and engine data. The platform displays this on a map dashboard, sends geofence and route-deviation alerts, and stores trip history for route replay and reporting.

Can fleet management software reduce fuel costs?

Yes. Fleet management software reduces fuel costs by optimizing routes, detecting idling and fuel theft, monitoring driver behavior (harsh acceleration, speeding), and scheduling preventive maintenance. These combined typically cut fuel spend by 15–25%.

Does the platform support driver management and safety?

Yes. The platform tracks driver behavior scores — acceleration, braking, cornering, speeding, and idling — and identifies coaching needs. Safety managers can review trip incidents, deliver targeted training, and reduce accident rates across the fleet.

Is fleet management software compliant with ELD and HOS regulations?

Yes. The platform supports ELD (Electronic Logging Device) integration and HOS (Hours of Service) tracking, generating compliant logs for DOT and other regulatory requirements. Automated alerts help prevent violations before they occur.

What size of fleet is the platform suitable for?

The platform scales from small fleets of 10 vehicles to enterprise fleets of thousands. Smaller fleets typically start with GPS tracking and maintenance scheduling, while larger fleets add dispatch optimization, telematics, and analytics dashboards.
