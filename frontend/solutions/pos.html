FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About POS

Quick answers about modern POS systems, payments, inventory sync, and multi-store management.

What is a modern POS system?

A modern POS goes beyond a cash register. It processes payments, tracks inventory in real time, manages customer profiles, and delivers analytics across your entire business. Cloud-based POS systems also let you manage multiple stores from anywhere.

Which payment methods does the POS support?

Our POS supports cash, credit and debit cards, UPI, mobile wallets, and other digital payment methods. All payment types are recorded in one place, so reconciliation and reporting stay simple.

Does the POS sync inventory across multiple stores?

Yes. Every sale updates inventory in real time across all store locations and sales channels. You can see stock levels, low-inventory alerts, and product movement from a single dashboard.

Can the POS work offline?

Yes. Our POS continues to process sales offline and syncs automatically when the connection is restored — so no lost sales during internet outages.

Is the POS suitable for restaurants and service businesses?

Yes. The POS supports retail, restaurants, cafes, salons, and service businesses. It handles table management, split bills, tips, and service-specific workflows — not just product sales.

What reports does the POS provide?

The POS generates real-time reports on daily sales, top-selling products, staff performance, payment breakdowns, and inventory movement. Reports can be filtered by store, date range, or product category.
