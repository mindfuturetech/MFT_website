Frequently Asked Questions About Portfolio Management

Quick answers about PMS software, AI insights, asset tracking, security, and tax optimization.

What is a Portfolio Management System (PMS)?

A Portfolio Management System (PMS) is software that lets financial institutions and investors track, manage, and optimize investment portfolios. It consolidates stocks, bonds, mutual funds, and other assets into one view, with real-time valuations, risk analytics, performance attribution, and automated client reporting.

How does AI improve portfolio management?

AI enhances portfolio management by analyzing market data, portfolio composition, and historical performance to identify trends, suggest rebalancing actions, and flag risk exposures. This helps advisors make faster, data-driven decisions that would otherwise require hours of manual analysis.

Does the PMS support multiple asset classes?

Yes. The system supports equities, bonds, mutual funds, ETFs, fixed deposits, real estate, and alternative investments. All holdings are consolidated into a single portfolio view, with performance tracked across asset classes.

Can clients access their portfolios directly?

Yes. The system includes a secure client portal where investors can view their holdings, returns, statements, and performance reports in real time. Access is protected with bank-grade encryption and role-based permissions.

How does the PMS handle tax optimization?

The system calculates capital gains across short-term and long-term holdings, identifies tax-loss harvesting opportunities, and generates tax-ready reports. This helps advisors and investors minimize tax liabilities while staying fully compliant.

Is the Portfolio Management System secure?

Yes. The PMS uses bank-grade encryption, role-based access control, multi-factor authentication, and complete audit trails. All data access is logged, and client information is isolated per account.
