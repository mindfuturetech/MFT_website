FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About AI Agents

Quick answers about autonomous AI agents, workflow automation, and how they integrate with your systems.

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is an autonomous software system that perceives its environment, makes decisions, and takes actions to achieve specific goals. Unlike simple automation, AI agents can reason through multi-step tasks, use tools and APIs, and adapt based on outcomes — often without human input.

How do AI agents differ from chatbots?

Chatbots respond to messages in a conversation. AI agents go further: they plan and execute multi-step workflows, call external tools and APIs, make decisions based on context, and complete entire tasks end-to-end. A chatbot answers; an agent acts.

What workflows can AI agents automate?

AI agents are ideal for multi-step workflows such as research and data analysis, lead qualification, report generation, customer support resolution, invoice processing, and cross-system data synchronization. Any task that requires reasoning across multiple steps and tools is a fit.

Can AI agents integrate with our existing systems?

Yes. AI agents connect to your CRM, ERP, email, databases, and third-party APIs through secure tool integrations. They can read data, trigger actions, and update records across systems while following your business rules and permissions.

Are AI agents secure and reliable?

Our AI agents run with role-based access control, audit logging, and human-in-the-loop approval options for sensitive actions. Every agent action is traceable, and you can define guardrails that limit what each agent is allowed to do.

How do AI agents learn and improve over time?

AI agents analyze the outcomes of their actions — what succeeded, what failed, and why — and use that feedback to refine future decisions. Over time this improves accuracy, reduces errors, and increases the share of workflows completed without human intervention.
