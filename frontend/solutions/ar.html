Overview

What Is AR?

Augmented Reality (AR) overlays digital content — images, instructions, 3D models, data — onto the real world through a smartphone, tablet, or AR headset. Unlike VR, AR enhances what you actually see.

Real-Time Overlay

See digital data layered on top of physical objects in real time.

Hands-Free Guidance

Remote Collaboration

Experts can see what field workers see and guide them live.
