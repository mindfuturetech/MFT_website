FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Augmented Reality

Quick answers about AR for business, training, field service, remote collaboration, and hardware requirements.

What is Augmented Reality (AR)?

Augmented Reality (AR) overlays digital content — images, instructions, 3D models, and live data — onto the real world through a smartphone, tablet, or AR headset. Unlike VR, which replaces the real world entirely, AR enhances what you already see.

How can AR improve field service and maintenance?

AR gives technicians step-by-step visual overlays on the equipment they're repairing, plus live remote assistance from experts. This reduces errors, speeds up repairs, and can cut equipment downtime by up to 40% — while reducing travel costs since experts can guide from anywhere.

Does AR work on standard smartphones and tablets?

Yes. Our AR solutions run on modern iOS and Android smartphones and tablets. For hands-free field work, they also support AR headsets and smart glasses. No specialized hardware is required to get started.

Can AR be used for employee training?

Yes. AR training overlays interactive, step-by-step guides onto real equipment and processes. Employees learn by doing, with visual prompts in their field of view. This typically makes onboarding 3x faster than manual-based training.

How does AR help with remote collaboration?

AR remote assistance lets a field worker share their live camera view with a remote expert. The expert can draw annotations directly in the worker's field of view, point to specific components, and guide the repair in real time — as if they were standing next to them.

What industries benefit most from AR?

AR delivers the fastest ROI in manufacturing, field service, logistics, healthcare, retail, and education. Any industry with complex equipment, remote workers, or training-intensive processes sees immediate benefits from AR-guided operations.
