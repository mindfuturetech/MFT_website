FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Retail Solutions

Quick answers about omnichannel retail software, inventory sync, POS integration, and loyalty programs.

What is omnichannel retail software?

Omnichannel retail software connects your in-store, online, and mobile channels into one unified system. Inventory, orders, customers, and loyalty data stay in sync everywhere — so a sale in-store updates online stock instantly, and customers get a consistent experience across every touchpoint.

How does smart inventory help reduce stockouts?

Smart inventory uses AI-driven replenishment to analyze sales velocity, seasonality, and lead times, then suggests optimal reorder points. This keeps popular items in stock while minimizing overstock — typically improving inventory efficiency by around 40%.

Can your retail software integrate with our existing POS?

Yes. Our retail platform integrates with your existing POS, e-commerce store, accounting, and CRM through standard APIs. You can keep your current POS hardware and unify it with online channels, inventory, and customer data.

What loyalty features are included?

Our loyalty engine supports points, tiered rewards, personalized offers, and referral programs. It uses purchase history and customer behavior to automatically suggest offers that increase repeat purchases and average order value.

Can the platform manage multiple store locations?

Yes. You can manage any number of store locations from a single dashboard — with per-store inventory, staff roles, sales reporting, and centralized pricing. This lets you grow from one store to a national chain without changing systems.

What analytics does the retail platform provide?

You get real-time dashboards on sales, top products, customer behavior, store performance, staff productivity, and channel comparison. Reports can be filtered by store, date, product category, or customer segment — with export options for accounting and BI tools.
